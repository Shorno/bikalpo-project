# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Retailers and shop owners who need to quickly browse warehouse inventory, verify available quantities and pack sizes, and order stock for their retail stores.

## Product Purpose
A clean, efficient B2B wholesale storefront that allows retailers to view inventory details, search/filter by category, and easily place bulk or wholesale orders.

## Brand Personality
Tech-precise, clean, mature, and professional (inspired by utility-focused platforms like Stripe, Linear, and Figma). It conveys reliability, accuracy, and efficiency.

## Anti-references
- Loud, over-saturated colors
- Generic consumer e-commerce templates with heavy box shadows
- Side-stripe borders on list items or cards
- Identical repetitive grid boxes without layout hierarchy
- Excessive decorative modals or animations

## Design Principles
- **Information Density with Clarity**: Group details (price, MOQ, quantity) neatly to maximize readability without cluttering the screen.
- **Sober & Trustworthy Neutrals**: Use tinted neutrals and subtle borders for layout boundaries rather than thick dividing lines or heavy background colors.
- **Intentional Hierarchy**: Elevate important structural items (like category tabs, search input, and in-stock badges) using typography weight contrast and precise spacing instead of container boxes.
- **Responsive Simplicity**: Maintain a consistent typographic grid on both mobile and desktop screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Clear high-contrast text and labels.
- Structured, semantic HTML elements for easy navigation.
- Accessible font scales and keyboard-friendly buttons.
